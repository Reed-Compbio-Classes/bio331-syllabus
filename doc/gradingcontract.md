### Grading Contract

The work in this class consists of 4 programming assignments, 3 exams, and 6 research deliverables (13 graded pieces total; see the [types of work page](typesofwork.md)). The 13 pieces are designed so that if students complete them (or if they complete all but one, but have another excellent piece), they will earn a B. If you earn a B in the course, you will have learned _a lot_ about computational systems biology. 

Additionally, there are 4 items graded as "Submitted / Not Submitted" and 13 labs graded as "Present / Absent" (17 low-stakes pieces total). Most (if not all) of these should be submitted/marked - there are opportunities to make up labs you cannot miss. 

The table below is based off of requirements for a B. There are 8 opportunities for an E (programming assignments, exams, and the final research deliverable) - earning one or more "excellents" will improve your grade beyond a B if you have completed all pieces. If you partially complete pieces, miss assignments, or miss extra labs, that reduces your grade. However, one "Excellent" will bump up a partially complete piece to a complete piece. There is no adjustment for missed work.

**To interpret the table**, if you have an "excellent" piece you can swap it to bump a "partially complete" piece up to make it "complete." Then use the number of M/P/C/E pieces to find your grade. The number of missed pieces are considered first.

| Earned Grade | Assignment Requirements | #  Missed Graded Pieces Allowed (13 total) | # Missed Low-Stakes Pieces Allowed (17 total) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | 
| A | Requirements for a B and at least three pieces are Excellent: one must be a programming assignment (P1-P4), one must be an exam (E1-E3), and one must be the research deliverable R6 | 0 | 0 |
| A- | Requirements for a B and at least two pieces are Excellent | 0 | 1 |
| B+ | Requirements for a B and one piece is Excellent | 0 | 2 |
| **B** | **Complete the 13 graded pieces<br> (P1-P4, E1-E3, R1-R6)** | **0** | **3** |
| B- | Requirements for a B, except 1 piece is partially completed | 0 | 4 |
| C+ | Requirements for a B, except 2 pieces are partially completed | 0 | 4 |
| C | Requirements for a B, except 4 pieces are partially completed or missing | 1 | 5 |
| C- | Requirements for a B, except 5 pieces are partially completed or missing | 2 | 5 |
| D | Requirements for a B, except 6 pieces are partially completed or missing | 3 | 6 |
| F | Requirements for a D are not met | >6 | >6 |

## Assessment Communication
We will discuss your course plans at multiple points throughout the semester. In the first week, you will share your reasons for taking the course, what success in the course means to you, and your plans for achieving that success. You are welcome to talk with me any time about your progress in the course and how it aligns with your goals. Communication is key.
